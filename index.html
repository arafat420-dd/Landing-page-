💡 Tech Tips

5 Simple Tips to Improve Your Phone Life

  1. Turn on auto-backup for photos so nothing gets lost if your phone breaks.
  2. Disable unused app permissions — improves both privacy and battery life.
  3. Use dark mode at night to reduce eye strain and save battery on OLED screens.
  4. Clear app cache monthly to keep your phone running smoothly.
  5. Enable Find My Device in case your phone gets lost or stolen.
